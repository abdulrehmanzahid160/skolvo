The $9 Agent tier is deliberately below [Teal+ at $29 per 30 days](https://www.tealhq.com/pricing)
and [Huntr Pro at $40 monthly](https://help.huntr.co/en/articles/10714568-plan-types-and-pricing).
Those products include more mature features, so their prices are ceilings for this introductory
offer, not exact comparables. For CampusNova, published Pakistan alternatives span
[PKR 1,500/month for a small academy](https://pakedusystem.pk/solutions/tuition-academy-management-software)
to [PKR 7,499/month for a 300-student school](https://ims.metagenious.com/); scope and student
limits vary substantially. For SignalWatch, [QARACloud lists $49.90/month for a broader FDA
suite](https://www.qaracloud.com/) and [SignalFDA starts at $89/month for a monitored-code
product](https://signalfda.com/). The proposed $29 applies to one selected service, not all three.

## Safepay cost check

Safepay currently publishes [2.9% + PKR 30 for domestic card charges and an additional 0.3%
for international cards](https://safepay.com.pk/pricing). Its page says subscriptions have no
extra subscription-specific fee. Confirm the actual merchant agreement, currency conversion,
and tax treatment before treating any price as net revenue. Revisit Agent pricing if AI or
support costs make $9/$4 unprofitable, and define enforceable usage limits before launch.
